EverlookDB Auctions: Price Feed brings other players' newest auction house scans into EverlookDB Auctions, so your prices are fresh even when you haven't scanned yourself, out in the world as much as at the auction house.

### How it works
Players who use the EverlookDB App upload their scans to EverlookDB.com. The app brings the newest prices for each auction house you play on (your realm, your faction and the neutral one) into this addon. Your next /reload or login picks them up.

### In the game
EverlookDB Auctions uses whichever scan is newer, yours or the feed's, for every item. Prices from the feed say so: "other players' scan · 12 min ago". The 30-day low and median use both. Your own saved data is never changed. The status bar at the auction house shows "Price Feed: 12 min old".

### What you need

- EverlookDB Auctions (required).
- The free EverlookDB App from everlookdb.com fills this addon. Without it the addon stays empty and EverlookDB Auctions works on your own scans, as before.

No player names are in the feed or anywhere in EverlookDB Auctions.
